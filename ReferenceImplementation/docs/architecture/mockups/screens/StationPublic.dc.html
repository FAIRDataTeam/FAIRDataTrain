How to send a train here

1. Pick a train in a Train Handler — a query, API call or container from a garage this station trusts.
2. Your request is matched against the controller’s condition; some datasets need the controller’s approval (you see the deadline).
3. The train runs here, inside the sandbox; results are inspected before anything leaves.
4. You receive aggregates and a provenance record; obligations (e.g. deletion) are listed with them.

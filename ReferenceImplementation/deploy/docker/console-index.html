FAIR Data Train — testbed

Two stations, a Train Depot, a metadata registry and a Train Handler, running against each other. The two stations are the point: one is in a network that lets a machine conclude a negotiation, the other is also in one that does not.

Stations

  • University station (fictional) Automated decisions permitted — the M1 run is granted without anybody being asked
  • Oosterlicht MC station (fictional) Also in stroke network Oost, where a machine may not decide: see the approvals queue
  • Public catalogue What the University station publishes to anybody, with no credential at all

The rest of the ecosystem

  • Train Depot The authority for a train: its class, parameters, input requirement and payload digest
  • Metadata registry An index of what the others publish — and explicitly not a trust anchor
  • Train Handler The finished M1 run, its events and its envelope; trains found through the registry

Credentials

Three parties ask three different things of a station, and each has its own credential. The operator's surface — the visit list, the audit slice, the settings — is testbed-operator. Oosterlicht's approvals queue belongs to a data controller and is testbed-controller. Neither is a network credential, and neither grants anything about data.

Every station, party and dataset named here is fictional. Ports and addresses come from deploy/profiles/*.env.
